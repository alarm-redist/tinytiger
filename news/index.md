# Changelog

## tinytiger 0.0.11

CRAN release: 2025-10-24

- Adds support for 2025 TIGER geographies.

## tinytiger 0.0.10

CRAN release: 2025-04-30

- Adds support for 2024 TIGER geographies.

## tinytiger 0.0.9

CRAN release: 2024-06-04

- Adds support for 2023 TIGER geographies.

## tinytiger 0.0.7

- Wrap slow downloads in `\donttest{}`

## tinytiger 0.0.6

- Resolves a CRAN check error when a 503 occurs.

## tinytiger 0.0.5

- Adds support for 2022 TIGER geographies. No corresponding TIGER
  geography updates exist for
  [`tt_cbsa()`](https://alarm-redist.org/tinytiger/reference/tt_cbsa.md),
  [`tt_csa()`](https://alarm-redist.org/tinytiger/reference/tt_csa.md),
  [`tt_metropolitan_divisions()`](https://alarm-redist.org/tinytiger/reference/tt_metropolitan_divisions.md),
  `tt_new_england_city_division()`,
  [`tt_new_england_combined_areas()`](https://alarm-redist.org/tinytiger/reference/tt_new_england_combined_areas.md),
  or
  [`tt_puma()`](https://alarm-redist.org/tinytiger/reference/tt_puma.md).

## tinytiger 0.0.1

- Initial release
- Basic support for all TIGER geographies for the current year and broad
  support for years since 2010. Some functions support 2000 as well.
